# Recipe Box keyboard guide

This guide lists Recipe Box keyboard controls. Focus the relevant control before using a shortcut. `Ctrl` means the Control key.

## Moving between controls

- Press `Tab` to move forward and `Shift+Tab` to move backward through controls.
- Press `Enter` or `Space` to activate a focused button or option.

## Editing ingredients

- In the desktop ingredient table, press `Enter` on a cell to edit it. Type a printable character to start editing with that character.
- Press `Ctrl+ArrowUp` or `Ctrl+ArrowDown` in an ingredient row to move it up or down.
- In ingredient suggestions, use `ArrowUp` and `ArrowDown` to change the highlighted suggestion. Press `Enter` to accept it, or to commit the typed value when there is no suggestion list.
- Press `Tab` in an open suggestion list to accept the active suggestion and move to the next ingredient cell, when suggestions have been typed or navigated.
- In an inline ingredient text editor, press `Enter` to commit or `Escape` to cancel.
- In measurement picklists, use `ArrowUp` and `ArrowDown` to open or move through options. Press `Home` or `End` to select the first or last option. Press `Enter` or `Space` to open the list or choose the active option. Press `Escape` to close it.
- In the mobile ingredient editor, press `Enter` in a regular input to apply the row, or `Escape` to close the editor. In the ingredient suggestion input, `Enter` accepts the suggestion or typed value, and `Escape` cancels that edit.

## Editing instructions

- Type `#` to show ingredients already on the recipe. Typing a query searches the account-wide ingredient catalog, including when `#` is inserted directly before an existing word. Use `ArrowUp` and `ArrowDown` to select an option, then press `Enter` to link it or create an unmatched ingredient.
- Press `Ctrl+.` in an instruction to insert `°` at the caret, replacing any selected text.
- Press `Enter` in an instruction to insert a new step after it and focus the new step.
- Press `Shift+Enter` to insert a line break within the current instruction.
- Press `Ctrl+ArrowUp` or `Ctrl+ArrowDown` to move the current instruction up or down.
- Press `Escape` while ingredient suggestions are open to close them and keep the typed `#` text. Otherwise, it discards a new step or restores an existing step to its text when editing began.
- Instruction numbers reflect their current order and update after reordering.

## Recipe picklists

- Press `Escape` while a recipe picklist is open to close it and return focus to its trigger.
