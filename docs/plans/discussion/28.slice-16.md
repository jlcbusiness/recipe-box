## Resolution

The reader refinements are implemented and specified in [Slice 16](../slices/16-cooking-views.md)
and the [visual design guide](../visual-design.md). Presentation and Sort cycle
through their options using active-state icons; the reader toolbar keeps its
sort-direction slot stable, places Scale and Cook mode beside the Ingredients
heading, and the Ingredient-first table separates Volume and Weight. Cook mode
uses a dismissible wake-lock warning and an unlabeled checkbox column when
Ingredient-first is active.

Slice 16 remains incomplete while the full E2E, accessibility, and
repository-wide Biome gates have failures. Current verification results are
recorded in the slice brief; the roadmap status remains unchanged until those
gates pass.
