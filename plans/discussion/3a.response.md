One Privacy Detail

Public recipe images must be readable through the public recipe page without exposing the whole image bucket. Use private Supabase Storage with short-lived signed image URLs generated only after the recipe’s public-link check passes. Do not put recipe images in a broadly public bucket.

History Detail

Recipe history restores need to be transactional: restoring a version restores the recipe, ingredient rows, steps, references, and publication links together. Keep history as an append-only audit log plus periodic complete snapshots; diffs alone become brittle when settings values are merged or deleted.

The remaining decisions can safely wait until wireframes: exact metadata density, Recipe Tin illustration, library grid/details layout, and the vocabulary/order of verdict options.
