I updated all six planning documents and replaced the roadmap's eight slices with 25 thinner ones. Several of your answers needed interpretation, so I've listed my assumptions below for you to confirm.

## Your questions

**Slice size (item 2).** The original slices were too thick. Slice 2 alone held the editor, ingredients, mentions, trash and print, and slice 7 combined unrelated public links and history. The roadmap is now slices 0–24. It adds an engineering baseline, splits sign-in from invites, and breaks recipe core into several slices. Images, settings, density, cooking views, search and saved views, install/offline, and export/import are each their own slice. I also added "standing rules" that apply to every slice (history, edit conflicts, trash, security, accessibility).

**History mechanism (item 3).** Every recipe change needs a log entry (who changed what, before and after). The log can be written by the database itself (triggers) or by our own code. The history screen is late in the roadmap, but the logging has to exist from the first recipe save. Otherwise you would have to go back and retrofit every earlier slice. The roadmap now chooses the mechanism in slice 4 and gives every later slice the job of logging its own writes.

**Editor (item 4).**
- My first line meant that when you pick "flour" after `#`, the editor must store a real link to the flour row, not just the letters. It also needs a dropdown as you type, and instructions as plain text for search.
- A spike (I called it a "proof slice") is a short throwaway experiment to answer one technical question. Slice 3 compares editor libraries on markdown, `#` on mobile and accessibility, and its output is a decision-log entry.
- Instructions and notes are now both markdown, and `#` is the trigger on every platform.

**Limits (item 6).**
- Vercel's rate-limiting rules are available on all plans, and the Hobby plan allows one rule per project. The 60 requests per minute per IP I wrote in for public recipe pages is my own suggestion, not a documented figure.
- Supabase's default for sign-up, sign-in and recovery is 30 requests per 5 minutes per IP.
- Supabase's built-in email sender allows 2 emails per hour, so you need custom SMTP for password recovery.

**Either/or sign-in.** It isn't quite either/or. Supabase automatically links a Google sign-in to an existing account with the same email. The docs call linking to an unverified email a pre-takeover risk. Invite-only largely removes that risk, because you type the email on the invite. **Do you want one login method per account, or should an invited person be able to use either?** That feeds the invites slice (slice 2). With invites, the "skip email verification" checkbox may also be redundant.

**Edit conflicts.** Each recipe carries a version number. If you save from a stale copy (for example, the phone changed it while the desktop was open), the save is rejected and you choose between reloading and keeping your edits.

**Decision log.** It is a short table of decisions and the reason for each. Its job is to stop a future session from re-arguing something already settled. I added entries for everything decided in this round.

**Dark mode.** It was not a decision. "Light/dark themes" appeared in the contrast notes in `visual-design.md` and the reference. I removed those mentions, made the first release light-only, and listed dark theme as deferred.

**Rows with both volume and weight.** A row like `1 cup / 120 g` fits two size groups. I made it sort by its volume. Say if you'd rather it behave differently.

## My interpretations to confirm

- Unit systems are called "US customary", since UK imperial pints and gallons differ. Per-ingredient unit overrides are view-time only and not saved to the recipe.
- Count has no unit. This removes the count-unit picklist, and I changed the examples to eggs and a 14 oz coconut milk. How do you want "1 bunch cilantro" or "3 sprigs thyme" to work? Detail or the ingredient name could carry it.
- Magazine recipes have no standalone URL. It lives on the `+ Site` listing, which allows at most one site per recipe and only on magazine-primary recipes.
- In-use deletes require a replacement, but optional fields may also be cleared. Ingredients always require a replacement.
- A trashed recipe's URL returns 404, and a purged one returns 410. That needs a record of purged IDs, which I added.
- Recipe photos are now in the roadmap (slice 11) and reference §2.3, with a list of what that slice must settle.
- I fixed the flour example: 1 cup of all-purpose flour is about 120 g, not 254 g, across all documents.
