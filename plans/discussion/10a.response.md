## Non-standard unit type

Yes, add one. "Bunch", "sprig", "clove", "head", "stick" and "pinch" are real measures with no weight or volume, and they don't fit Count (a bare number), Unmeasured (a phrase like "to taste"), or Detail. I suggest five types:

| Type | Example | Unit |
| ---- | ------- | ---- |
| Volume | `1 cup` | Fixed list |
| Weight | `120 g` | Fixed list |
| Count | `2` eggs | None |
| Informal | `1 bunch` cilantro | Your own list (bunch, sprig, clove, head, stick, pinch, dash) |
| Unmeasured | `to taste` | Approved phrases |

An Informal amount scales (`2-3 cloves` at 2x gives `4-6 cloves`) but never converts and never takes part in density. It sorts in the Count group. Packaging words such as can and bag stay out of the seed list, as you decided.

I'd avoid "thing" as the name, since it reads oddly in a dropdown. "Informal" or "Portion" would work. Tell me which you prefer and I'll make the edits. They bring back the unit list under Settings and update the reference, spec, data model, roadmap and decision log.
